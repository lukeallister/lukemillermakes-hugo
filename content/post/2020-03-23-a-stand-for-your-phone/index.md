I previously built a [Roubo phone stand](/2019/07/roubos-phone-stand/) that gets used all the time. But phonestands are so useful that I have more.

A lot more.

![](images/r_IMG_20200322_205246935.jpg)

I designed this one in [OpenSCAD](https://www.openscad.org/), a free text-based CAD program, and it is fully parametric. You can adjust the size and the angles to ridiculous degrees. But this is the one that I like and use. I think I have 4 of these around the house and I'm still tempted to print another.

They're great for video calls, or just having your phone visible but not in your pocket.

Here's the scad: [phone-stand](/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/phone-stand.scad)

And this is the stl of the one that I use all the time: [phone-stand-stabilized](/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/phone-stand-stabilized.stl)

I hope you give it a print and enjoy.
