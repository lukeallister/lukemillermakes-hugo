I want a bass that is comfortable, cool-looking, but most of all _mine_. I have glued up a blank of jatoba and have begun sketching out the shape of the body itself. There's a funny-looking knothole that I'm designing around. Rather than worry that this will ruin my bass, I am preparing myself to embrace it. Perhaps it will become a design element (or at least not interfere with electronic placements).
